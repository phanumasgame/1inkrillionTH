## Membership evidence and scope

### Zoo animals

- San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance public animal catalog was read across pages 0–11. The pages collectively cover ordinary animal groups and named species, including binturong, cheetah, clouded leopard, capybara, sun bear, panda, giraffe, gorilla, koala, macaw, marmoset, otter, red panda, sloth, tapir, tiger, toucan and zebra. The base source links through to the relevant animal pages.
- Queens Zoo's official domestic-animals exhibit supplies alpaca and llama membership. Zoo animals include domestic/farm exhibits as well as wildlife.
- The Zoological Park Organization inventory and Songkhla Zoo's public animal pages were consulted for Thai animal names, including gibbons and binturong. The inventory PDF later timed out during a direct HTTP retry; it is not evidence of animals being on display today.
- Generic names such as bear, monkey, snake, turtle, tiger and owl are intentionally accepted. This question does not ask formal zoological species or a current inventory of a single Thai zoo.
- Python/งูเหลือม/งูหลาม are one broad accepted family-level answer for this casual wording. They are not claimed to be the same zoological species.
- The collection is curated rather than every possible zoo animal.

### All original 151 Pokémon

- Both Thai and English official Pokémon portals expose an initialPokemons roster in their public Next.js page payload.
- Filter used: zukan_sub_id === 0 and zukan_id from 0001 through 0151 inclusive. Both rosters produced 151 records. Thai canonical labels and English aliases were paired by dex number.
- Alternate regional/mega forms and generations after 151 are outside the visible prompt scope.
- Nidoran♀ and Nidoran♂ remain separate species records. Their aliases explicitly specify female/F or male/M. Plain Nidoran is intentionally not an alias of either.
- Farfetch'd/Farfetch’d/Farfetchd and Mr. Mime/Mr Mime are supported. Reviewed common Thai spellings include ปิกาจู/ปิกาชู, ลิซาดอน, เนียส, โคดั๊ก, อีวี่ and มิวทู; these are acceptance conveniences, not replacements for the current official Thai canonical names.
- Dex numbers alone are not accepted.

### Video games

- Sources include publisher/developer websites for Garena, Gravity, Zepetto, Dragonfly/GG, PlayPark, Nintendo, Riot, Mojang/Microsoft, Roblox, Supercell, HoYoverse, EA, Moonton, King, SYBO, Imangi, Konami, Square Enix, The Tetris Company and Bandai Namco. The final question sources list the public catalog or title links.
- RoV, Free Fire, Ragnarok Online, Point Blank, Special Force, Pangya, Audition and PUBG are deliberately represented alongside worldwide PC/console/mobile/indie games.
- Nintendo's historical Pangya Wii listing verifies the game series, rather than claiming the old Thai online service is currently running.
- The prompt explicitly accepts games or series. Thus franchise answers such as Mario, GTA, FIFA/EA Sports FC, Counter-Strike, Pokémon, Final Fantasy and eFootball/Pro Evolution Soccer are accepted without forcing a numbered release.
- The PUBG entry groups the Battlegrounds and Mobile family for this prompt. This is an acceptance decision, not a claim that PC and mobile executables are identical.
- FF is not assigned because it can mean both Final Fantasy and Free Fire. Thai context supplies aliases such as RO, PB, SF, ปังย่า, ออดิชั่น, พับจี, วาโล and มายคราฟ.
- A mistaken candidate Steam ID for CrossCode resolved to Albert and Otto in the primary response and was excluded. A Final Fantasy XIV appdetails request failed; Final Fantasy uses Square Enix's official portal instead.
- The 103-entry set is curated, not a complete catalog of every released game.

### Ball sports

- Included both spherical and oval balls, metal boules and the woven takraw ball. The hint discloses this scope without listing sports.
- Football/futsal/beach soccer, basketball/3x3, volleyball/beach volleyball and rugby/sevens include explicitly named competition variants.
- Federation rules/catalogs cover racket sports, snooker/billiards/pool, boccia, croquet, netball, goalball, padel, korfball, dodgeball, teqball and golf. IWGA's official sport/discipline catalog adds racquetball, floorball, billiards and petanque coverage.
- The Sepak Takraw Association of Canada official rules and Sport Singapore equipment guide explicitly describe the spherical one-layer woven ball.
- Field hockey is accepted as ฮอกกี้สนาม/Field hockey. Ice hockey's puck and badminton's shuttlecock do not meet this question's ball criterion. Plain ฮอกกี้ is not an exact alias because it does not disambiguate the two.
- Croquet's Thai Wikipedia candidate โครเกต์ redirects to the food ครอแก็ต. It is deliberately null, not used as a popularity proxy.

### Musical instruments

- Yamaha's primary guide covers common woodwind, brass, guitar, violin, piano, organ, drums, mallet percussion and celesta.
- Philharmonia's own player guide supplies viola, cello, double bass, harp, keyboard and additional orchestral instruments.
- Fine Arts Department materials supply Thai strings, winds, drums, percussion and northern/Isan instruments; Hohner covers melodica and harmonica; Deering and Gold Tone supply banjo and mandolin.
- UNESCO's official Angklung entry verifies the bamboo instrument and collaborative playing. No article paragraph is copied into the bank.
- General names and subtypes are intentionally accepted separately (guitar/acoustic/electric/classical; drum/drum kit; ranat/ranat ek/ranat thum; saw/saw duang/saw u). English flute belongs to the concert flute entry, while Thai khlui is separate.
- Instruments are not restricted to a school orchestra or Thai ensemble.

### Phone brands

- Product pages from 29 official manufacturer sources cover 31 brand records; Xiaomi's official catalog covers Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO separately.
- Nokia is verified with HMD's Nokia 3210 product/specification pages, HMD with its own smartphone catalog.
- Historical brands are allowed by the visible hint. BlackBerry's official device end-of-life FAQ and LG's official closure announcement verify past phones; no claim of current hardware sales is made.
- HTC's annual report explicitly identifies the U24 pro smartphone.
- Consumer naming aliases accepted: iPhone→Apple, Galaxy→Samsung, Pixel→Google, Xperia→Sony, Moto/Razr→Motorola, Zenfone/ROG Phone→ASUS. Redmi and POCO aliases are kept separate from Xiaomi.
- A manufacturer brand page is evidence of factual phone-brand membership, not popularity, market share, recommendation, local availability or price.

## Score and proxy limitations

- All six tier IDs are valid; each question has exactly one designated krillion answer. Some questions do not use tooclever because that tier is optional.
- Every tier/reason is editorial familiarity in Thai context. There are no invented response counts, percentages, survey results or claims that a gem is empirically the least common answer.
- Ten wikiTitle proxies are intentionally populated: ยีราฟ (สกุล), หมีขอ, พิคาชู, ไมน์คราฟต์, แร็กนาร็อกออนไลน์, เซปักตะกร้อ, กีตาร์, โอโบ, ซัมซุง, โมโตโรลา. Canonical title, redirect and entity identity were checked via the Thai Wikipedia API. The other 468 values are null.
- Samsung and Motorola are broader company/brand pages, not a phone-only measure. Giraffe is a genus page, explicitly matching this question's generic animal label. These are contextual candidates for the parent proxy system; this part does not calculate pageview-derived popularity or real human answer frequencies.
- Wikipedia was used only for entity/proxy matching, not as the authority for factual membership.
